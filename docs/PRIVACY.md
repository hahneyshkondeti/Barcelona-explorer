# Brisa — Barcelona privacy statement (submission draft)

Brisa — Barcelona is an offline sightseeing driving game. The game does not require an account and does not transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no advertisements or analytics SDKs and does not request your device's GPS location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos.

The app stores your virtual car's last safe position, discovered landmarks and sound/frame-rate preferences on your device. These coordinates describe the car inside the game, not your physical location. Deleting the app removes its local app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Street, building, tree and place records are bundled snapshots from open geographic datasets. They are not collected from your device. Data updates currently arrive through a replacement app build.

Apple may handle App Store purchases, downloads, diagnostics and device backups under its own policies and your device settings. Information you choose to send directly when requesting support is outside the game's offline save system.

Before publication: the app owner must add a support/privacy contact and effective date, review this statement against the final signed binary, and publish it at a stable public URL for App Store Connect.

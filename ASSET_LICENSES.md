The CC0 material files were downloaded unchanged; the shader tints and scales them. They are generic plaster and asphalt surfaces, not photographs of Barcelona façades. In-game credits identify ambientCG and the license.

OSM is visibly attributed in the driving interface and credits. The database includes original OSM IDs, record edit dates, available survey/check dates, snapshot date and source hash. Download dates are not presented as business verification dates. Unknown addresses are left blank and described as missing.

The municipal address table was investigated but is not incorporated: its street-code/name join was unavailable. No claim of municipal address verification is made. No Google map imagery, Street View capture, GTA asset or proprietary building mesh is used.

## Transit and traffic furniture

`data/city/infrastructure.json` is derived from the existing dated `data/source/barcelona.osm.gz` snapshot, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Its metadata retains the retrieval date; each record retains its OSM identity, edit date, original coordinates and tags. Original low-poly poles, shelters, metro/bus markers and traffic-sign geometry are generated by `scripts/street_furniture.gd`; no Google imagery, agency logos, advertising, or scans are included. These are illustrative representations, not replicas of actual station entrances, shelters or sign installations.

## Plaça de Catalunya and Plaça d’Espanya

`data/city/public_spaces.json` retains OSM feature IDs and dated geometry, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Catalunya's square derives from relation 1732345 (outer way 19964084); its twin fountains from relation 21286243; the compass-rose area from way 532893860. Espanya's island is way 126832504 and its fountain basin way 126832508. Garden, water and bench records keep their individual IDs.

The procedural geometry in `scripts/public_spaces.gd` and water shader are original. Architectural facts were checked against [Turisme de Barcelona's fountain description](https://www.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/cat/page/1214/font-monumental-de-la-placa-espanya.html), [Jujol's architectural record](https://www.jujol.org/placa-espanya), and [Turisme de Barcelona's Catalunya description](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/1241/placa-de-catalunya.html). No photographs, imagery textures or scanned meshes are bundled. The simplified sculpture, architectural proportions and decorative paving are interpretations, not an exact reproduction. Water is a simulated visual display.
